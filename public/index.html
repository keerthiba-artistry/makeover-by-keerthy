Bridal Answers & Guidance

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find key insights on HD vs Airbrush makeup, booking lead times, outstation wedding travel, saree draping, and organic mehendi care.

Why is Keerthiba Artistry recognized as the best bridal makeup artist in Chennai?
Keerthiba Artistry brings 4+ years of specialized bridal expertise and over 500+ successful transformations. We use 100% premium international luxury cosmetics (M.A.C, Charlotte Tilbury, NARS, Huda Beauty) to deliver long-lasting, customized HD and Airbrush makeovers paired with professional saree draping, mehendi, and hair design.
What is the main difference between HD Makeup and Airbrush Makeup for South Indian weddings?
HD Makeup uses high-pigment, light-reflecting liquid cosmetics applied with precision brushes for a seamless, soft-focus finish ideal for close-up 4K photography. Airbrush Makeup uses a compressed air mist for a weightless, transfer-resistant, and 16+ hour sweatproof finish perfect for humid weather and long outdoor ceremonies.
Do you travel outstation for destination weddings across Tamil Nadu and abroad?
Yes! Master Artist Keerthiba and her team travel across Tamil Nadu (Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem), South India (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kerala), and internationally for luxury destination weddings.
How far in advance should I book my bridal makeover package?
To ensure availability on peak South Indian wedding and Muhurtham dates, we recommend reserving your bridal slot 3 to 6 months prior to your event date.
Is saree box pleating and pre-stitching included in the bridal styling services?
Yes, we offer complete professional saree pleating, pre-stitching, and pin-securing for Kanjeevaram silk sarees, Madisar, and lehengas to ensure maximum comfort and crisp, picture-perfect pleats all day long.
